BACKGROUND & MOTIVATION

Problem Statement

Challenges in modern enterprise employee presence tracking

⚠️ Proxy Attendance

Traditional RFID cards and static printed QR codes allow colleagues to clock in on behalf of absentees by sharing photos or cards.

🏢 Hybrid Work Chaos

Managing work-from-home requests manually without hard capacity caps leads to understaffed offices or untracked remote workers.

🔒 Lack of Auditability

Manual attendance registers and unstructured spreadsheets lack cryptographic verification, revision history, and role governance.

KEY INNOVATION

Anti-Proxy Dynamic QR System

Preventing screen sharing, replay attacks, and proxy clock-ins

⏱️ 18-Second Expiry

Tokens generated on the server expire in 18 seconds. Screen photos taken on phone cameras become invalid before they can be forwarded.

⚛️ Atomic Consumption

Upon scan, MongoDB executes findOneAndUpdate({ used: false }) atomically. A token can only be consumed once.

📍 Location & Session Bound

Tokens are cryptographically bound to specific office reception kiosk locations, requiring authenticated mobile app scans.

BUSINESS LOGIC

Attendance Policy Engine

Deterministic time-window calculations in Asia/Kolkata (IST)

⏰ Check-In Windows & 9:30 AM Cutoff
  • Office Start Time: 09:00 AM IST.
  • Check-In Cutoff Time: 09:30 AM IST.
  • Grace Arrival Policy: Check-ins between 9:00 AM and 9:30 AM are marked PRESENT. Arrivals after 9:30 AM follow configurable policies (MARK_LATE, HALF_DAY, or ABSENT).
  • Duplicate Prevention: Prevents multiple check-ins on the same day for a single user.
🏡 Concurrency-Safe WFH Management
  • Hard Daily Quota: Configured to maximum 5 approved WFH employees per day.
  • Concurrency Safe: Validates approved request count before approving new requests.
  • Pre-approval Requirement: Employees cannot mark WFH attendance without an approved request.
  • Reason Requirement: Mandatory reason and decision audit comment.
